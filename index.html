How I used Custify and AI to cut down the manual work of keeping strategic account plans up to date, while making sure CSMs stayed in control of the final result.

Why I put this together

Deepgram asked applicants to share something they’d built that reduced recurring work. There wasn’t anywhere to cover this in the application form, so I thought I’d put together a short case study instead.

I’ve also used AI to help create this page, which felt like a good opportunity to put it to practical use again.

The example itself comes from my time at Solidatus, where I used AI alongside Custify to reduce the manual effort involved in keeping strategic account plans up to date.

01 / The challenge

Keeping a detailed account plan current should not mean rebuilding it every month.

At Solidatus, strategic account plans began as substantial Excel workbooks covering stakeholders, use cases, risks, SWOT, account notes and customer health. They were useful but required recurring manual effort to review changes across multiple sections.

As correspondence and account context accumulated, the administrative work of reconstructing what had changed competed with time better spent on customer decisions and engagement.

02 / The approach

Get the account information organised. Then use AI to help keep it current.

I worked on moving our plans out of spreadsheets and configured Custify extensively to represent the account-planning framework within the Customer Success platform rather than relying on the original spreadsheets. This brought account context and captured communications into a more central working view.

Structured context

Stakeholders, use cases, risks and health represented in the CS operating system.

Regular AI review

AI-assisted review on an approximately monthly cadence to surface material changes.

CSM ownership

Human review, correction and judgement before treating outputs as reliable.

03 / The workflow

A repeatable process, with the CSM making the final call.

This is how the review process worked. AI helped with the initial review but the CSM checked the findings before using them.

01

Customer signals

Account data, correspondence and interactions accumulate.

02

Structured account context

Custify provides the working account view and planning categories.

03

AI-assisted review

AI reviews recent information and highlights relevant changes.

04

Proposed changes

Emerging risks, positive signals and health context are identified for review.

05

CSM validation

The CSM checks accuracy, corrects gaps and applies customer judgement.

06

Account action

The validated view informs account planning and next steps.

04 / What changed

Less time pulling information together. More time acting on it.

The recurring review shifted from manually reconstructing changes across a multi-section plan to reviewing an AI-assisted first pass. Potential signals included declining or increasing usage, approaching renewals, new champions and developing account risks.

Before: manual reconstruction
  • Review account correspondence and usage context
  • Work through multiple planning sections
  • Identify changes and refresh notes individually
After: AI-assisted first pass
  • Review recent account information with AI
  • Flag potential changes for the relevant plan areas
  • Validate findings and focus on customer action
The CSM remained accountable.AI helped prepare and categorise information. The CSM remained responsible for validating the account record and deciding what action to take.
05 / Outcome and status

The point was to remove repeat work, not remove the CSM.

I did not build a standalone AI product. I improved the way we managed account plans in Custify and used AI to handle much of the initial review. That gave CSMs a better starting point for checking what had changed and deciding what to do next. The process was in use when I left Solidatus. I know the company later ended its Custify agreement to reduce costs, so I am not claiming the workflow is still running today.

Based on work I carried out at Solidatus. No confidential customer details or estimated time savings are included.
